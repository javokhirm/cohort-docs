By default, only owners and admins can add fee plans.

<RoleHint />

## Add the fee plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In the sidebar, select **Fee Plans**, then select **New fee plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the plan and choose its branch">
    Enter a **Plan name** you'll recognize later, such as *Monthly tuition* or *IELTS group*. Choose a **Branch**, or leave **Shared — all branches** selected to make the plan available everywhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price">
    Under **Billing cycle**, choose **Monthly** to charge a fixed amount every period regardless of how many sessions were held, or **Per session** to bill only for sessions actually held. Enter an amount greater than 0 — labeled **Amount (UZS)** or **Price per session (UZS)** depending on the cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" />
</Steps>

## What's next

A new plan isn't attached to a course yet. [Add a course](/en/admin/courses/add-a-course) and choose it there so the course's groups start billing on it. Or go back to [setting up a new center](/en/getting-started/first-time-setup).
