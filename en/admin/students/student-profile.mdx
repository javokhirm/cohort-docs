Every student has their own page in the Staff console, with their details, groups, attendance, marks and money in one place. By default, owners, admins and managers can open it.

## Find a student

In the sidebar, select **Students**. Search by name or student code, then select the student to open their page.

The list shows **Active** students at first. To see students with another status, choose **All**, **Inactive**, **Graduated** or **Suspended** above the list.

## The top of the page

The top of the page shows the student's name and status, their student code, branch and phone number.

The ⋯ button holds the actions for this student:

- **Edit**: change the student's details, status or password. See [Edit a student](/en/admin/students/edit-a-student).
- **Sign in as student**: open the Student console as this student. See [Sign in as a student](/en/admin/students/view-as-student).

Both are available to owners and admins by default.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Personal details |
| **Guardians** | Parents and guardians connected to the student |
| **Enrollments** | Every group the student has been in, and any standing discounts |
| **Performance** | Attendance and per-lesson marks |
| **Grades** | Assessment results |
| **Billing** | The student's invoices |
| **Wallet** | The wallet balance and its transactions |

### Overview

**Personal details** shows the student's **Date of birth**, **Address**, **Gender** and **Enrollment date**: the day the student was added to Cohort.

### Guardians

Each guardian with their relation to the student, name and phone number. The **Primary** and **Pickup OK** badges mark the primary guardian and who may collect the student. Select a guardian to open their details. See [Manage guardians](/en/admin/students/guardians).

### Enrollments

One row per group the student has been enrolled in:

- **Group** and **Course**
- **From**: the date the student was enrolled
- **Status**: **Active**, **Suspended**, **Dropped**, **Completed** or **Transferred**

Use the filter above the table to show only one status. Owners and admins also see a **Standing discount** column: see [Give a standing discount](/en/admin/students/standing-discount).

To enroll the student in another group, open that group and select **Enroll students**.

### Performance

Attendance and the marks teachers give for each lesson. Narrow it down with the filters at the top: a period (**All time**, **This month**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**), a group (**All groups** or one of the student's groups) and an attendance status. **Clear filters** resets them.

- **Attendance rate**: the share of the student's classes they attended, out of the classes where attendance was taken. **Late** counts as attended. A red badge appears when the rate is below your center's attendance threshold.
- **Average session mark**: the average of the student's lesson marks for the period.
- **Session history**: one row per lesson, with the **Date**, the **Session** topic and group, the **Attendance** (**Present**, **Absent**, **Late** or **Excused**) and the **Mark**. **Not marked** means the student attended but the teacher hasn't given a mark.

The attendance status filter only narrows **Session history**. The two figures at the top always count every lesson in the chosen period and group.

### Grades

Results of assessments the teacher has published, newest first. Per-lesson marks aren't listed here: you'll find them on **Performance**.

### Billing

Every invoice issued to the student, newest first, with the **Invoice** number, **Date**, **Total** and **Status** (**Draft**, **Unpaid**, **Partial**, **Paid**, **Overdue** or **Void**).

This tab is for viewing only. To record a payment or change an invoice, open it from **Invoices** in the sidebar.

### Wallet

The student's **Wallet balance** and **Recent transactions**, with buttons to top up or correct the balance. Owners, admins and managers see this tab by default. See [Student wallet](/en/admin/students/student-wallet).
