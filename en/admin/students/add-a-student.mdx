By default, owners, admins and managers can add students. Only owners and admins can set a student's password.

<RoleHint />

## Add the student

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In the sidebar, select **Students**, then select **Add student**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter personal details">
    Fill in **First name** and **Last name**. **Date of birth** and **Gender** are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the branch">
    Under **Contact**, choose the **Branch**. **Phone** and **Address** are optional: students sign in with a student code, not a phone number, so a student without a phone can still use Cohort.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a guardian (optional)">
    Under **Guardian**, enter the **Parent / Guardian phone**.

    - If someone in your center already has this number, Cohort shows who it is. Check that it's the right person, then select **Connect guardian**.
    - If the number is new, enter the guardian's first and last name in **Guardian name** and choose the **Relation**.

    To add the student without a guardian, leave the phone blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enroll in a group (optional)">
    Under **Initial enrollment**, choose a **Group**. This section only appears when the branch you chose has groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" />
</Steps>

## Let the student sign in

A new student can't sign in until you set a password for them. To set one:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the student">
    In the **Students** list, select the student.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the student">
    At the top of the student's page, select the ⋯ button, then select **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a password">
    Under **Access**, enter a **Password** of at least 8 characters, then select **Save student**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Give the student their student code and password. They sign in at [student.cohort.uz](https://student.cohort.uz): see [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in).
