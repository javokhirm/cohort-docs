title: "Issue an invoice"
description: "Turn a draft invoice into one the student can see and owes, so it counts toward their balance."

import RoleHint from "/snippets/en/role-hint.mdx";

An invoice you [create by hand](/en/admin/invoices/create-an-invoice) is saved as **Draft** — it isn't visible to the student and doesn't count toward what they owe. Issuing it makes it **Unpaid**: visible to the student, and due by the date you set.

By default, owners and admins can issue invoices.

<RoleHint />

## Before you start

- The invoice must be **Draft**. Invoices Cohort creates automatically skip this step and start as **Unpaid** already.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    In the sidebar, select **Invoices**, then select the draft invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue it">
    Select **Issue invoice**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Once issued, you can no longer change the due date or add line items. To add a discount or record a payment, use the actions on the invoice itself.
</Note>
