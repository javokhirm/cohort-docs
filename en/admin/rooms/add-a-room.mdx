By default, only owners and admins can add rooms.

<RoleHint />

## Before you start

- A room always belongs to a branch. If you haven't added one yet, [add a branch](/en/admin/branches/add-a-branch) first.

## Add the room

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In the sidebar, select **Rooms**, then select **New room**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the room details">
    Enter a **Room name**, then choose the **Branch** the room belongs to. Enter a **Capacity** — how many people fit in it. **Type** defaults to **Classroom**; choose **Lab** or **Online** instead if this room is different.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" />
</Steps>

## What's next

Choose this room when you [add a group](/en/admin/groups/add-a-group) that meets in it. Or go back to [setting up a new center](/en/getting-started/first-time-setup).
