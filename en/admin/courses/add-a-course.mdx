A course is the subject or curriculum offering a group is based on — for example "IELTS Prep" or "Math Grade 5". By default, only owners and admins can add courses.

<RoleHint />

## Add the course

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In the sidebar, select **Courses**, then select **New course**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the course's name and branch">
    Enter the **Course name**, and choose the **Branch** it belongs to, or select **Shared — all branches** to offer it everywhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a fee plan">
    Choose the **Fee plan** every group under this course will bill on. A **Shared** course only offers shared plans; a branch course offers shared plans plus that branch's own.

    <Tip>
      If nothing's listed, select **Create fee plan**. It takes you to the Fee Plans page and away from this form, so anything you've entered here is lost — finish adding the course afterward. See [add a fee plan](/en/admin/fee-plans/add-a-fee-plan).
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the optional details">
    **Level**, **Default duration (weeks)** and **Description** help tell similar courses apart later, but none are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" />
</Steps>
