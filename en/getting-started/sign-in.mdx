Each role signs in to its own app. Choose yours:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Staff">
    For owners, admins and managers.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Staff console">
        Go to [admin.cohort.uz](https://admin.cohort.uz).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your details">
        Fill in **Phone number** and **Password**, then select **Sign in**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Teachers">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Teacher console">
        Go to [teach.cohort.uz](https://teach.cohort.uz).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your details">
        Fill in **Phone number** and **Password**, then select **Sign in**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Students">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Student console">
        Go to [student.cohort.uz](https://student.cohort.uz).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your details">
        Fill in **Student code** (for example, A1234567) and **Password**, then select **Sign in**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  You can change the interface language with the language switcher on the sign-in page.
</Tip>

## Where to get your password

Cohort has no self-service password reset.

- Your center's owner or an admin creates staff and teacher accounts and gives you your password. If you forget it, ask them to set a new one.
- Students get their student code and password from their education center. If you forget either, ask the center.

## If you can't sign in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“Invalid phone number or password.”">
    The phone number or password doesn't match any account. Check the number, make sure Caps Lock is off, and try again. If it still fails, ask your center's owner or an admin to set a new password for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="“Invalid student code or password.”">
    Enter the student code exactly as your center gave it to you. If it still fails, ask your education center for your code and a new password. For staff helping a student, see [A student can't sign in](/en/troubleshooting/student-cant-sign-in).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="“You do not have the required role for this action.”">
    You're signing in to an app that isn't for your role, for example a teacher signing in to the Staff console. Open the app for your role: see [Apps and roles](/en/getting-started/apps-and-roles).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="“Too many failed sign-in attempts for this account.”">
    After 10 failed attempts within 15 minutes, signing in to that account is paused for up to 15 minutes. Wait, then try again with the correct details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="“This education center is suspended.”">
    Your education center's access to Cohort is paused. Contact your center's owner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
